# Shorts selection heuristics (transcript-first)

## Pick segments that will actually perform

- **Hook is explicit in 1–2s**: a question, warning, or contradiction (“…인 줄 알았는데”, “근데”, “결론부터”).
- **One topic per clip**: avoid clips that cross “다음으로/마지막으로” section boundaries unless you trim or bridge with narration.
- **Fast payoff**: problem → evidence → takeaway fits inside 15–60 seconds.
- **Proof shot exists**: something you can show (device close-up, settings screen, numbers, comparison).
- **Emotion + usefulness**: reactions (“진짜/말이 안/대박”) + actionable advice (“~하면 ~됩니다”, “~하시는 분들은”).

## Simple editing template (works for tech reviews)

1. **0–2s**: on-screen hook text (big) + immediate proof shot
2. **2–10s**: context in 1 sentence (trim filler, jump cuts)
3. **10–(end-2)s**: core explanation + 1–2 emphasized keywords/scores/numbers
4. **Last 2s**: takeaway checklist + optional CTA (“주의/추천/결론”)

## Narration bridges (when you need to cut harder)

Use a single sentence to connect two kept beats:
- “정리하면, 원인은 __고 해결은 __입니다.”
- “결론부터 말하면 __만 하면 됩니다.”
- “이건 __때문이라서 __한 분들은 특히 주의하세요.”
